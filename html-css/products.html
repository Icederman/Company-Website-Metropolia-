  • Creatine

    Creatine is one of the most researched sports supplements available. It helps your muscles produce quick energy during short, intense efforts like lifting and sprinting, which can support strength gains and better performance over time. A daily serving of 3 to 5 grams is the usual recommendation, and it can be taken at any time of day.

  • Whey Protein

    Whey protein is a fast-digesting protein made from milk, rich in the amino acids your muscles use to recover and grow. It's a convenient way to reach your daily protein target, especially after a workout or when whole food isn't practical. Mix a scoop with water or milk for a quick shake.

  • Fish Oil

    Fish oil is a source of omega-3 fatty acids, mainly EPA and DHA, which are linked to heart, brain, and joint health. Many athletes take it as part of a balanced diet, particularly if they don't eat fatty fish regularly. Look for a product that lists its EPA and DHA content clearly.
